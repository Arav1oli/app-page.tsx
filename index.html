OPEN DECISIONS

CallOptionsCosts
First run size 10 units vs 50 10 prices us at the top of the benchmark. 50 gets us inside it but is $92k out the door before a sale.
Construction Welded box · folded wrap · monocoque Monocoque gives the sealed green side with no gap. Adds ~$135/unit and needs a roller who can do 3.2 mm at 1450.
Finish Powder · ceramic · enamel · 316 coastal Powder is cheapest and the default. Ceramic bonded is the Gozney look at roughly +$55/unit over powder.
Supplier Toshine vs multi-quote One quote is not a market. Two more needed before any commitment.
