WHAT THE LEADS ARE ASKING FOR

Feature requests attached to inbound leads, 4 Sep: santa maria grill, flat top / plancha, auto rotating spit, main rotisserie spit, churrasco skewer attachment, V-shape grill. Rotation is being asked for repeatedly and is not currently in the Rev G pack.

OPEN DECISIONS

CallOptionsCosts
Sample first Take their sample offer, or go straight to a batch Toshine want to build and photograph a sample before bulk. That is the right process and it de-risks the $420. It is also not costed anywhere yet.
First run size 10 units vs 50 At the custom price the margin holds at either. At the adders price, 10 is too thin. 50 is ~$66k out the door before a sale on the custom basis.
Rotation In the launch spec, or an accessory Leads keep asking for spits and auto-rotation. Not in Rev G. Brand guidelines already name main spit and churrasco skewers as working modules.
Construction Welded box · folded wrap · monocoque Monocoque gives the sealed green side with no gap. Adds ~$135/unit and needs a roller who can do 3.2 mm at 1450.
Finish Powder · ceramic · enamel · 316 coastal Powder is cheapest and the default. Ceramic bonded is the Gozney look at roughly +$55/unit over powder.
Supplier Toshine vs multi-quote One quote is not a market. Two more needed before any commitment.
